### 4.2 Fee currency, calculations and quantity effects

- **Verified conditional spot fee-currency specification:** the Enum Definitions section “Spot Fee Currency Instruction” [S8] branches on whether `makerFeeRate` is positive. Its positive branch charges Buy in base currency and Sell in quote currency. In the nonpositive branch, maker orders use Buy quote/Sell base, while nonmaker orders use Buy base/Sell quote. This is the documented conditional tree, not a claim that any account has a negative fee or rebate. It must not be simplified into “spot fees always use USDT.”
- **Verified reconciliation fields:** execution history documents `execQty`, `execPrice`, `execValue`, `execFee`, `feeCurrency`, `isMaker`, `feeRate` and millisecond `execTime` [S7, response fields]. These support an eventual per-fill reconciliation design; they do not provide a verified complete fee formula/rounding rule or actual fill costs. Current USDT-perpetual fee-currency and rounding/calculation details remain UNKNOWN without the relevant product rules and no account-specific values are retrieved.
- **Documented current constraints schema:** instruments describe derivative `minOrderQty`, `qtyStep`, `minNotionalValue` and `tickSize`; spot uses `minOrderAmt`, with older spot `minOrderQty` marked deprecated [S2, response fields]. Actual minimum sizes/steps are intentionally unqueried and UNKNOWN. These are not trading parameters selected by this task.
- A fee charged in base currency can reduce net spot inventory. Equal gross order quantities do not necessarily leave a matched hedge. Lot rounding, partial fills and later rebalances require actual net base-unit accounting, not just equal quoted notionals.
- A maker flag or a resting order is not a guaranteed fill, rebate or spread capture. Bid/ask spread, slippage, queue position, partial execution and emergency unwind costs remain unmeasured. Order-book snapshots omit RPI orders according to their specification [S18, introduction]; displayed depth is not a complete executable-liquidity guarantee.

### 4.3 Four-fill accounting structure

| Fill | Required fee/currency evidence | Unresolved execution/cost issue |
|---|---|---|
| Spot entry | Applicable spot maker/taker rate; actual fee asset and net acquired base quantity | Spread/shortfall and fee deducted from inventory; first-leg risk. |
| Perpetual entry | Applicable linear USDT rate, contract multiplier, actual filled short quantity and fee asset | Matching base quantity, collateral availability and partial second leg. |
| Spot exit | Effective exit-date rate, actual quantity and fee currency | Fee/tier changes, exit liquidity and residual inventory. |
| Perpetual exit | Effective exit-date rate, closed short quantity and fee currency | Close execution, margin pressure and forced-unwind cost. |

A conceptual ledger would sum each actual fee converted consistently into a common numeraire, add funding receipts/payments and paired basis PnL, and subtract financing, rebalancing and any separately measured execution shortfall. If actual bid/ask fill prices already determine realized price PnL, the same spread/slippage must not be deducted again. Financing costs are not funding cashflows; margin pledged is committed capital, not automatically an expense. No numerical tariff, spread, return estimate or break-even threshold is introduced in this report.

The official FAQ also warns that Closed PnL entry/exit prices are influenced by fees and are not actual order execution prices [S27, “Why are my Closed PNL prices inaccurate?”]. Reproducible fill accounting cannot assume that summary provides untouched executable prices. The cost-ledger requirements are clearer, but **reproducible cost accounting is not yet satisfied** without actual effective rules and a separately authorized point-in-time data plan.

## 9. Remaining blockers

The smallest set of **evidence bundles** that could change the feasibility decision, without starting market research, is:

1. **Current product/legal entitlement bundle:** accessible authoritative restricted-jurisdiction, KYC, legal-entity and exact spot/USDT-perpetual/API permission terms applicable to Argentina, plus clarity on which account-specific facts would later be necessary. Do not request identities, connect accounts or assume permission from the routing guide.
2. **Complete economics/survival rule bundle:** official funding rate-to-cashflow formula, payer/receiver sign, settlement inclusion/valuation, cap/revision rules, fee schedules and effective-date/discount/rounding terms; plus margin/offset/collateral/liquidation/funding-debit/financing rules sufficient to specify a conservative two-leg budget. Schema-only evidence cannot substitute for these rules.
