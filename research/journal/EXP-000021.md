## Objective

configuration on 15m candles. This was not a parameter sweep and did not
attempt to identify stronger settings.

## Hypothesis

## Deterministic interpretation

`FEE_DOMINATED` is the primary failure. Positive gross expectancy (`0.069345`)
was smaller than fee expectancy (`0.079972`), turning net expectancy negative
(`-0.010627`). Gross PF (`1.209526`) fell to net PF (`0.971714`). Moderate
follow-through weakness and temporal instability are secondary observations.

The short baseline showed measurable directional asymmetry relative to frozen
long `EXP-000020`, but this is discovery knowledge only and does not change the
`BASELINE_REJECT` verdict.
