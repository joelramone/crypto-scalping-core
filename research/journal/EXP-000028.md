## Objective
Check whether the frozen daily trend rule of HYP-DAILY-TREND-BTC-001 improves risk-adjusted returns over buy-and-hold in traditional asset classes.

## Strategy
Position = mean of three votes `close > SMA(n)` for n = 50, 100, 200 trading days, on adjusted prices. Long or flat, entry at the next open, 0.1% fee per unit traded, cash at 0%. Annualized with 252 days.

## Results

Evaluation window 2005-10-18 to 2026-10-29.

| Asset | Strategy CAGR | Strategy Sharpe | Strategy max DD | B&H CAGR | B&H Sharpe | B&H max DD | Pass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SPY | 6.8% | 0.68 | -22.3% | 11.3% | 0.67 | -55.4% | yes |
| EFA | 4.5% | 0.45 | -24.0% | 6.1% | 0.40 | -60.9% | yes |
| TLT | -0.5% | 0.00 | -48.4% | 2.8% | 0.27 | -50.2% | no |
| GLD | 5.3% | 0.45 | -41.4% | 10.2% | 0.63 | -45.3% | no |

- 2008: SPY strategy -8.3% vs -36.8% buy-and-hold; EFA 0.0% vs -41.8%.
- On equities the rule's Sharpe is roughly equal to buy-and-hold's (SPY 0.68 vs 0.67). It cuts drawdowns by more than half, but gives up about 4.5 points of CAGR on SPY.
- On TLT and GLD it loses to buy-and-hold on both Sharpe and return, with similar drawdowns (frequent whipsaws, 0.26–0.33 of capital in total fees).
- Cash at 0% understates the strategy. SPY was out of the market about 25% of the time, so T-bill interest would add roughly 0.5–1 point per year. This does not change the verdict.

## Lessons Learned
- The rule mainly works as drawdown protection on equity-like assets that have occasional deep bear markets (BTC, SPY, EFA). It does not help on gold or long bonds.
- It does not raise long-run returns on equities; its value is a smoother path.
