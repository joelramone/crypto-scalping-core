## Objective
Test whether long/flat daily trend following on BTC spot beats buy-and-hold on a risk-adjusted basis, with ETH as an unchanged control.

## Strategy
Position = mean of three votes `close > SMA(n)` for n = 50, 100, 200 (0, 1/3, 2/3 or 1), decided after the close and held from the next open. Cash earns 0%. Fee 0.1% per unit traded.

## Timeframe
1d

## Frozen Parameters
- sma_lengths: [50, 100, 200]
- execution: next open
- fee_rate: 0.001
- cash_yield: 0
- drawdown limit for the verdict: 0.6 x buy-and-hold max drawdown

## Lessons Learned
- The rule improved risk-adjusted returns in both halves and on ETH, but its drawdown was 65% of buy-and-hold's, not the required 60% or less.
- These results are in-sample on a widely known history and must not be read as evidence of a live edge.
- Experiment closed under preregistered anti-tuning rules. The drawdown limit cannot be relaxed after the fact.
