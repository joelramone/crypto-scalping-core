## Objective
Evaluate the single frozen baseline for HYP-AGGRESSOR-FLOW-PERSISTENCE-001 on Dataset A, both legs.

## Hypothesis
Persistent one-sided taker flow (kline `taker_buy_base_volume` proxy), observed only after it happens, predicts a signed continuation that survives a one-bar delay and fees.

## Dataset
Dataset A: Binance USD-M BTCUSDT 1m, 2023-01-01 to 2023-04-15 exclusive (149,760 rows), resampled to 29,952 complete 5m candles.

## Strategy
Signal at the onset of 3 consecutive 5m candles with imbalance `(2 * taker_buy - volume) / volume` >= +0.10 (LONG) or <= -0.10 (SHORT).

## Timeframe
5m
